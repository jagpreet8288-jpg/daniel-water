7-Stage Purification & Bottling Process

Every drop inside a Daniel 250ml bottle undergoes rigorous multi-stage filtration, mineral balancing, and untouched automated bottling.

STAGE 1 & 2

Sand & Carbon Filtration

Eliminates suspended particles, dust, chlorine, odor, and organic impurities from raw water.

STAGE 3 & 4

RO + Mineral Balancing

Reverse Osmosis removes heavy metals and excess TDS, followed by essential mineral addition for sweet taste.

STAGE 5 & 6

Micron & UV Disinfection

Sub-micron polishing and high-intensity UV sterilization neutralize 99.99% of waterborne pathogens.

STAGE 7

Ozonation & Auto-Sealing

Ozone treatment locks in long-lasting freshness before automated contactless filling into Virgin PET bottles.
